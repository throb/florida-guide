05

Wildlife & nature watch

What's moving out there right now, so you know what you're likely to see and what to leave alone.

Manatees

Summer scatter

The big winter herds have spread out for warm-season feeding. You'll still spot a few grazing in the rivers. Give them space, no touching, and idle speed in the bay.

Sighting odds nowFair

Scallops

Opens Jul 1

Citrus County's season opens July 1 and runs through late September. Snorkel the grass flats off Homosassa and Crystal River once the window opens.

Countdown to openAbout 4 weeks

Shorebirds

Active now

Ospreys, herons, spoonbills, and wading flocks work the marsh edges at dawn. First light on Ozello or Fort Island is the quiet show.

Best atFirst light

Wildlife notes follow FWC guidance. Seasons and rules can change, so check myfwc.com before you go.
